MICROSOFT'S 6 RESPONSIBLE AI PRINCIPLES
========================================

These are the philosophical guardrails Microsoft uses to guide AI development.

1. FAIRNESS
   - AI systems should treat all people fairly.
   - Avoid biased outcomes based on race, gender, age, or other attributes.

2. RELIABILITY & SAFETY
   - AI systems should perform reliably and safely.
   - They should behave as expected and have fail-safes.

3. PRIVACY & SECURITY
   - AI systems should respect privacy and be secure.
   - Handle data responsibly and protect it from misuse.

4. INCLUSIVENESS
   - AI systems should empower everyone.
   - Designed to benefit people with different abilities and backgrounds.

5. TRANSPARENCY
   - AI systems should be understandable.
   - People should know how and why decisions are made.

6. ACCOUNTABILITY
   - People should be accountable for AI systems.
   - Clear ownership and responsibility for AI outcomes.


RELEVANCE
---------
These principles are especially important when deploying AI in:
- Regulated industries (finance, healthcare, government)
- Products that make decisions affecting people (hiring, lending, healthcare)
- Enterprise systems where trust and compliance matter
